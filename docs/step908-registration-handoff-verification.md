# Registration list, translated detail workspaces and quotation readiness

## Corrected behavior

The main AI registration list now reads both saved page captures and saved URL-query captures. They keep distinct immutable references and use the existing category and promotion paths. Top select-all and clear control both product IDs and capture references. The current company is part of the read session, so a late response from a previous company cannot restore its rows. Captures sort by their saved time together. Verified URL save/archive acknowledgements refresh the shared list; an uncertain acknowledgement does not announce a completed save.

Automatic Stage 5 now opts into `shared-body-pixels-v1`. Final quotation saving and safe included peer workspace publication share one guarded transaction. The source workspace retains its option-bound text, crop and brush records. Included peers retain their photo identities, source identities and banners, and receive ordinary owned copies of the exact translated BODY pixels. They can open and edit those photos without borrowing another option's private text-layer authority. Excluded or manually divergent workspace documents are preserved. A divergent included peer, changed company/category/source, corrupt image or concurrent peer update blocks publication.

The command binds both the pre-commit peer proof and the post-commit proof. Missing save acknowledgements recover through reads. Reusing a completed publication re-reads its exact receipt and rejects subsequent peer edits instead of returning a stale cached completion. Existing requests without the publication policy keep their previous identities and behavior.

Stage 7 readiness now checks invalid optional populated fields and aggregate quotation constraints, including legacy validation fields. Modern review-only notes, valid optional blanks and explicit empty wire choices remain nonblocking. A blocked draft no longer tells the member it is ready to transmit.
